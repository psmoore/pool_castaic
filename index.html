Three places to look

“Can I swim laps at 5 on a Tuesday?”

The home page grid says yes: lap swim 4–8pm. The Team Sports page puts the Youth Water Polo Team in the pool 5–6. The Lessons page lists a 5pm Level 2 class, from a session that ended three weeks ago; the current one is on ActiveNet. Put together, lap swim, a lesson and water polo are all in the water at 5.

Here they're side by side on one calendar, so a swimmer sees what else is in the water before driving over. LA County runs more than fifty pool pages like this one, and each could show its calendar the same way.

Questions for the aquatics staff

Everything on these calendars comes from what the pool publishes. These are the places where two of its pages disagree, where a time isn't published, or where we had to make a call.

  • GapHow many lanes are left for laps at 5pm?
    From 4 to 8pm on weekdays the Fall grid shows lap swim. In the same hours the pool also runs a swim lesson every hour, the Youth Water Polo Team from 5 to 6, and Water Exercise from 6 to 7. No page says how the pool is divided.
  • ConflictThe Lessons page is a session behind
    The class list on the Lessons page is Session 1 (August 24 – September 4). The current classes, Session 3 (September 21 – October 2), are only on ActiveNet, and they add a 7pm Level 3 and a 6pm Level 4 that the page doesn't show.
  • GapSaturday, September 26
    ActiveNet lists Masters Family Water Polo from 8:30 to 11am. The Fall grid has lap swim 8–11 and Water Exercise 8–9 at the same time, and the Saturday lessons start at 9. Does the game take the whole pool?
  • GapWeekdays from 11am to 3pm
    Nothing is scheduled between morning lap swim and afternoon rec swim. Is the pool closed, or open for something not listed?
  • GapSwim Team, Dive Team and Artistic Swimming
    The Team Sports page lists the Swim Team for “Winter 2026” at 5–6pm, the same hour as water polo, and Dive Team and Artistic Swimming as TBA.
  • GapThe 50-meter pool is closed
    On September 25 the lifeguards said the 50-meter pool (8 long-course or 20 short-course lanes) is out of service, and lap swim, lessons, water exercise and rec swim all run in the 10-lane 25-yard pool. The center also has a 3-lane shallow pool and a splash pad. No page mentions the closure or a reopening date.
  • OursFive areas in the 25-yard pool
    No page says which lanes each program uses, so the 25-yard pool is split into lap lanes, a lesson area, a water exercise area, a team area and a rec swim area, so the calendar can show them side by side. Water polo is placed there too; unconfirmed. Lessons are on the calendar through Session 3 and the Saturday session; later sessions aren't posted yet.
